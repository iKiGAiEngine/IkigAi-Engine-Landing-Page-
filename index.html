Questions

The things owners ask first.

What does Ikigai Engine actually do?

Three things, in order. First we help you see your business clearly — pulling your scattered numbers, orders, and notes into one place so you can tell where time and money actually go. Then we automate the repetitive busywork that quietly eats your week. Then we bring in AI where it genuinely helps — reading documents, drafting the reports you dread, answering the questions you keep re-answering. Clarity first, then automation, then AI.

Do I have to replace the software I already use?

No. We map how you already work and connect the tools you’ve got — no rip-and-replace, no forcing you onto a new platform. The goal is to make your current setup flow, not to sell you a migration.

What’s the free operations audit — what’s the catch?

There isn’t one. It’s a focused 30-minute conversation where we find the spots where you’re losing time, repeating yourself, or guessing when you should know. You leave with a clear picture of what’s worth fixing first — whether or not you ever hire us. No pressure, no jargon.

What do you mean “everything is yours to keep”?

Exactly that. The systems, automations, and the “second brain” we build are yours — documented and handed over, built on tools you own (like Obsidian, which is free) rather than locked behind our subscription. If we parted ways tomorrow, everything keeps working and you keep all of it.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. You bring the deep knowledge of your business; we handle the technical side and explain everything in plain language. If a report needs building or two tools need to talk to each other, that’s our job — not yours.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Every business is different, so we scope it after the audit — you’ll always know the price and the plan before any work starts. Most engagements begin with one clear, high-impact win rather than a giant project, so you see value early instead of waiting months.
